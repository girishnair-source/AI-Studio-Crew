# Talking-head, influencer and presenter pieces (30 s – 3 min)

## Start from a brief
Know the one thing the viewer should repeat to a friend, who the presenter is talking to (one person, by name if you can), the platform, and the length. Gather the presenter's identity sheet and voice fingerprint.

## What makes it work
The presenter *is* the format; everything else is support. Viewers forgive rough picture and never forgive a flat voice. Talk to one person, not an audience; say the surprising thing first; never explain what you're about to say.

- The hook is the strongest sentence, cold, in the first two seconds. No greeting, no name, no "today we're going to".
- One idea per 15–20 s block, each with a *reason to keep watching* at its end.
- The ending is the payoff, then stop. CTA goes before the payoff.

## Craft
**Voice (the whole game).** Each block gets an intention from the DOP ("this is a confession", "this is the trick", "this is the dare") and inline tags; the read must *change shape* block to block — a quiet sentence, a punched word, a held pause. ≤ 30 words per 15 s clip; a crammed block always reads flat. Dailies run under the `presenter` monotony profile; a flagged block is a retake with stronger intention and fewer words, never a faster rate. Keep one voice fingerprint for the whole piece and quote it verbatim in every clip.

**Face & frame.** Eyes to lens, upper-middle, a little lean on the hook and the payoff. Vary the shot size at block boundaries (MCU ↔ CU) so cuts feel intentional, never two identical singles back to back. Hands in frame are good; gestures should land on the stressed word.

**Cuts.** Cut on the breath between ideas; hard cuts; a punch-in on the one word that matters. Jump cuts are fine in this format if the audio is continuous and the frame size changes.

**Captions.** A few words per card, one stressed word highlighted, lower-middle, off the face, on screen from frame one for muted feeds. Captions are a post layer with a font that has every glyph you use.

**B-roll & screens.** Show what is said (the app, the thing, the number), never decorate. Screen captures replace the face only for the seconds they are needed; come back to the face for the point.

**Sound.** Clean voice, room tone under everything, music low and out at the payoff. No whoosh on every cut.

**Ending.** Land the line, hold a beat, cut. No wave, no "thanks for watching", unless it is the presenter's recurring signature.

## By type
| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Explainer / tech | One demo beat on screen per idea; the presenter frames it, the screen proves it. |
| Opinion / hot take | The claim first, the strongest objection second, the answer third. |
| Pitch / keynote cutdown | One promise, one proof, one ask. Everything else is cut. |
| Series | Same opener and close every episode; vary the middle. |

## Rules
No teleprompter cadence. If a block *sounds* read, it is a retake, not a fade.
