# News bulletin and explainer-news (anchor-led, 2–30 min)

## Start from a brief
Know the rundown: segments in order with target durations, which anchor carries which, the languages, and the house look (desk, lower-thirds, ticker, stings). Gather the real assets: logo, colours, sound logo, supers template. Facts come from the script; the crew never invents a fact, a name, a number or a quote.

## What makes it work
Trust is the product. The same anchor, the same desk, the same sting every bulletin makes the content feel *true*; variation reads as fake. The viewer is half-watching: audio must carry the story alone, picture must carry the headline alone.

- One idea per segment; the super states it in ≤ 6 words.
- Lead with the newest fact, then context, then what happens next.
- Every number on screen is also said aloud; every name on screen is spelled from the script.

## Craft
**Anchor.** Cast once: identity sheet, wardrobe lock, voice fingerprint per language. Eyes to lens, upper-middle frame, consistent desk and light. Reads are *conversational authority*: brisk, warm, never flat — the DOP directs every paragraph with an intention ("this one is a warning", "this one is relief"). Monotony flag runs under the `presenter` profile; a flagged read is a retake.

**Rundown pacing.** Headlines fast (≤ 8 s each), lead story slow, explainers slower, kicker light. Pose the question before the explainer and hold two seconds. Never speed the voice to fit; cut words.

**Segments.** Anchor on-cam for the first and last sentence of each story; B-roll or graphics in between, showing what is said, never decorating. Graphics build one element at a time; labels sit on the object. Explainer visuals never put the narration on screen — two or three key words at most.

**Lower-thirds & ticker.** Deterministic post layer (Editor), never generated by the model: name + role in one style, headline supers ≤ 42 characters, ticker runs at reading speed. Clear of faces and the bottom 10 % (platform chrome). Captions off the anchor's face.

**Multilingual.** One script, one anchor, N languages: same shots, re-timed cut per voice (never speed the voice). Numbers, names and places are read from the script in each language and checked letter by letter.

**Sound.** Dialogue first; sting on segment boundaries at the same moment every bulletin; bed music only under headlines and the kicker, never under the lead. Broadcast loudness per delivery spec (−23/−24 LUFS for TV; −14 for social cutdowns).

**Cutdowns.** The bulletin is the master; each social clip is a new piece: one story, hook line first, captions on, 20–60 s, vertical reframe with the anchor upper-middle.

**Ending.** Sign-off is identical every bulletin — same line, same shot, same sting. The last frame is the brand.

## By type
| Video | Focus |
|---|---|
| Daily bulletin | Rundown first; shoot anchors in parallel per segment; package in rundown order. |
| Breaking | One fact, one anchor, no music, a plain super. Update the super, not the read. |
| Explainer segment | Problem → one concrete case → the rule → what it means for you. One idea per scene. |
| Weather / markets | Numbers on screen *and* spoken; graphics carry it, anchor frames it. |
| Social cutdown | One story, payoff line first, captions, out at the payoff. |

## Rules
Never invent facts, names, numbers, quotes or footage of real events. Generated visuals are labelled as illustration in the super when they depict a real event. Check every super against the script before delivery.
